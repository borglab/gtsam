GTSAM · Experimental planning

Learning to plan

16 control methods on 7 MuJoCo tasks from Kurtz and Burdick. All controllers share 5 initial conditions per task. Each episode lasts 20 seconds. The single cart-pole uses 15 paired starts for the 14 search controllers, with 128 trajectories per decision. Direct GPC and neutral input use the 5 common starts. The warm start advances by 20 ms and exploration narrows near balance. MPPI uses inverse temperature β = 8,192 on calibrated costs. Tsallis uses shape 2 and cutoff 0.000244141. Double cart-pole predictive sampling held 15/15 starts with 128 trajectories per decision. It centers 32 candidates on a local balance plan and adds a bounded quadratic terminal value when scoring candidates. All reported episode costs use the released task objective. All 15 double cart-pole controllers use the same five tuning and ten held-out starts. Search uses 128 trajectories per decision. Direct GPC uses one flow draw. The three learned controllers use residual actions around a feedback prior fitted on independent swing-up demonstrations. With zero residual the prior alone held 4/5 tuning and 9/10 held-out starts. Direct GPC held 10/15. GPC+ held 15/15 using physical rollouts. PO-MPC held 9/15 using learned-model rollouts. The 15 planar walker controllers in the paired study use 5 tuning and 10 held-out starts. Search methods score 128 trajectories per decision. Direct GPC generates one plan per decision. PO-MPC scores its candidates in the learned model. Smooth, Tube and Robust MPPI use part of the budget to select a warm start before the native update. The tracking graphs score posture and body motion at control knots. The batched MuJoCo searches penalize height and tilt excursions along each simulated path. Evaluation uses the released objective and a two-second hold test of height, tilt and speed. The humanoid uses 15 paired starts for all planners, with one direct plan or 32 search trajectories per decision. The crane uses 15 paired targets for all planners, with one direct flow plan or 128 search trajectories per decision. Tube and Robust MPPI use iLQR feedback on the inverted pendulum. Lower cost is better within a task. The task objectives use different units and scales.

Physical success criteria

Evaluate the final 2 seconds of each episode.

  • Inverted pendulum. Pole within 15° of upright, angular speed ≤ 1 rad/s.
  • Cart-pole. Pole within 15° of upright, cart within 0.25 m of center and moving at ≤ 0.5 m/s, pole speed ≤ 1 rad/s.
  • Double cart-pole. Both links within 15° of world upright, each absolute link speed ≤ 1 rad/s, cart within 0.25 m of center and moving at ≤ 0.5 m/s.
  • Push-T. Block within 0.03 m and 10° of its target pose.
  • Planar walker. Torso ≥ 0.9 m high and within 30° of upright throughout, with mean forward torso speed within 0.3 m/s of the 1.5 m/s target.
  • Luffing crane. Payload within 0.15 m of target with speed ≤ 0.25 m/s.
  • Humanoid stand-up. Torso IMU ≥ 0.8 m high and within 25° of upright.
